Speedrun (MCAT) - the three honest, ranged scores (from the SHARED Rust engine)
Deck: MCAT::Speedrun Starter | captured from the synced collection
Each score has a 95% range and a give-up rule (abstains when data is thin).

MEMORY      : 99%  (95% range 95%-100%)
              across 36 of 46 cards you've studied | sufficient_data=True
              = mean FSRS predicted recall of studied cards + Poisson-binomial band (give-up < 20 cards)

PERFORMANCE : 90%  (95% range 85%-94%)
              over 87 graded reviews across 36 studied cards | sufficient_data=True
              = online Elo mastery of THIS deck (Again=loss, Hard/Good/Easy=win); band narrows with #reviews (give-up < 30 reviews)

READINESS   : ABSTAINS (not shown) - the honest give-up rule.
              full_lengths=0 (need >= 5 scored full-length exams); sufficient_data=False
              A small, freshly-studied flashcard deck implies a misleadingly high number
              (provisional estimate would be ~525), so we DO NOT report it as readiness.
              Flashcard recall cannot substitute for a scored full-length; the app shows a blank until then.
